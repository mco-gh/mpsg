                    About this study guide

                    What it is

                    A free study guide for medical physics students preparing for their qualifying and board exams, such as the ABR Part 1 and Part 2 and end-of-program comprehensive exams. It covers radiation physics, dosimetry, radiobiology, radiation protection, diagnostic imaging, nuclear medicine, external beam therapy and brachytherapy.

                    What you can do

                    • Flashcards with spaced repetition. A few new cards a day, 15 by default, mixed with the ones you’re due to review. Rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy; cards you know well come back less and less often, just before you’d forget them.
                    • Practice questions. A quick 10, a drill on one topic, a timed 40-question mock exam, or a round of the questions you missed. Every answer comes with a worked explanation.
                    • The guide. High-yield notes for each topic, a formula sheet, key constants, and a table of the nuclides you need to know, all searchable.
                    • Progress. Your streak, a calendar of your study days, mastery by topic, and the reviews coming up.
                    • Set your exam date for a countdown. Use it offline, or install it on your phone or computer. There’s no account: your progress stays in this browser, and you can export a backup from Settings.

                    Keyboard shortcuts

                    • Cards: Space shows the answer, 1–4 rate it, Z undoes.
                    • Questions: A–D or 1–4 answer, Enter goes on.

                    A word of caution

                    The content was written carefully from standard references (Khan, Attix, Bushberg, Hall, the AAPM task group reports, ICRP, NCRP and NRC regulations), but it is a study aid, not an authority. Regulations and recommendations change; check the current documents, and your program’s own materials, for anything you rely on.
